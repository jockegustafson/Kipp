The profile is measured as the voice profiles of the lung model were measured on corpora: speech rate, how many pauses hold an audible in-breath, breath length and level, and voiced seconds between two breaths. Air and inhale speed follow from these by the lung model's rules; vital capacity cannot be heard (calibrate in tab 2). Without a word file, pauses come from voicing and syllables from loudness peaks: estimates, best with one voice on a close microphone and 30 s or more.

  1. Breathe out fully.
  2. Press Start, take your deepest breath, and then talk or read aloud for as long as you can without breathing.
  3. When you have to breathe, take a breath and press Stop.
Calibration measures the most air you use on one breath.

The voiced seconds on the one breath, times 0.11 l/s (the lung model's air per second of voicing), are the most air you use on a breath in the model's units. That air is taken to span 20–90 % of the vital capacity, which sets the model's capacity for your voice; the deep breath's length gives your full-effort inhale speed. Model values for the planner, not a lung-function test.
